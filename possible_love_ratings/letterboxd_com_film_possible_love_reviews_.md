The class drama was more centered than any romantic yearning to me, but I understand why reviewers led with that as the main takeaway.

★★★★½Liked[Watched by **Elijah\_Quinn**](https://letterboxd.com/elijah_quinn/film/possible-love/)01 Oct 2026

Beautifully poetic cinema, as expected with Lee’s works. A story that unravels with novelistic intent, centered around people’s relationship to labor as much as it is their relationship to each other; most compelling is the dynamic between Ye-ji, a first-time documentary filmmaker, and one of her subjects, Mi-ok. Lee navigates the relationship of these two and their partners with such beguiling and graceful control, the final act becomes immeasurably moving as all of the finely detailed intentions come into focus. One of the year’s (and possibly the decade’s) exceptional films.
